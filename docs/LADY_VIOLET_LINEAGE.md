# The Lady Violet Lineage

## Status

**Historical development lineage · source inventory pending · not automatically current game canon**

*The Legend of Lady Violet* is an earlier original story in the development lineage of *Violet's Revenge*. Violet's later investigation concerns the discovery of that history and the point at which an inherited story becomes materially real.

This records current authorial interpretation. Primary artifacts, dates, spelling, and publication history must be inventoried before claims are labeled verified provenance.

## Core conflict

Lady Violet and Sebastian oppose the same oppressive order from unequal positions.

- Sebastian is free to rebel and can judge refusal as an individual moral act.
- Violet controls resources but has been made responsible for everyone who may suffer if she refuses the marriage arranged around those resources.
- The promised King knows her participation is needed to rebalance the kingdom, even though the marriage would be loveless and does not express her free will.
- The system—not necessarily a single malicious person—is the central antagonist.

Their partnership becomes meaningful only if resistance redistributes risk. Sebastian must help build alternatives rather than treating visible rebellion as the only authentic resistance. Violet must convert responsibility into collective capacity rather than permanent self-sacrifice.
