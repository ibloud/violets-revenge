# Relationship to The RIM and Urban Alien Adventures

*The RIM* is understood as the genesis archive for Urban Alien Adventures: a story-delivery system in which narrative appears in advertisements, artifacts, and background media before the audience understands who the players are or how the fragments connect.

The Violet lineage can use that discovery grammar without making *The RIM* and *Violet's Revenge* mechanically identical.

## Discovery pattern

1. Players encounter forensic evidence that appears local to a case.
2. Recurring names, symbols, obligations, and locations imply an older pattern.
3. Lady Violet and Sebastian emerge as historical actors rather than decorative lore.
4. Violet recognizes that the lineage concerns her identity and present choices.
5. The inherited story becomes a live system the player must confront.

Specific RIM artifacts do not become canon until this repository records their promotion. Source dates, rights, and collaborators must be verified before publication.

The layered identity and reality discovery associated with *The Nines* may be named as structural inspiration only. It does not imply affiliation, endorsement, license, or adaptation rights.
