# Violet's Revenge — Role in the Ecosystem

## Classification

**Original gothic-forensics game branch · inherited-story investigation · active prototype**

*Violet's Revenge* is where the Lady Violet lineage is rediscovered through evidence and becomes consequential in the present. Its repository controls current game canon; historical documents provide provenance and design questions, not automatic canon.

## Connections

- *The Legend of Lady Violet* provides the ancestral political conflict.
- *The RIM* and Urban Alien Adventures provide a distributed discovery method.
- *Battle the Beast* demonstrates a separate nonlinear narrative experiment.
- *Veiled Dominion* shares abstract concerns with power, restraint, consent, and collective consequence while remaining a separate rules authority.
- PIXIE provides reflective and political commentary; it is not automatic game canon.
