# Ren / Violet Collaboration Record

> **Status:** Historical/project record and rights boundary. It is not a contract, license, ownership determination, or statement of Ren's approval.

## Why this record exists

This repository contains an original *Violet's Revenge* concept alongside development references to Ren's **"Violet's Tale."** The two works must remain distinguishable in the repository record.

The project may document the history of the idea, the existence of a proposed collaboration, and the distinction between the project character and a third-party artist/work. It must not convert that history into implied permission.

## Collaboration state

The repository records that Ren's work was a creative reference in the project's development history and that a possible collaboration/use was discussed.

The public repository does **not** establish:

- that Ren participated in developing *Violet's Revenge*;
- that Ren approved the character, story, music, or public presentation;
- that Ren licensed any recording, composition, lyrics, name, likeness, or other protected material;
- that this repository owns or controls any Ren work;
- that an unresolved collaboration creates indefinite control over the project's unrelated original work.

If the parties later reach an agreement, the agreement—not this repository text—controls the authorized uses.

## Rights and provenance boundary

Keep these questions separate:

| Question | Repository treatment |
|---|---|
| Who created the third-party work? | Attribute the source factually. |
| Who owns/controls it now? | Verify from the applicable rights record; do not infer from a public profile. |
| Was permission granted? | Record written permission only. |
| Was a collaboration discussed? | Record the documented project history. |
| Is a license in force? | Record the executed license and its scope. |
| Can the project proceed without a participant? | Treat this as a project/relationship question, not an automatic ownership transfer. |
| Can the project use the participant's work anyway? | No; obtain the applicable permission/license first. |

## Exit / continuation principle

A collaboration can have a defined participation window without making a person's participation an indefinite condition of another creator's ability to continue unrelated original work.

Where a collaboration remains unresolved, keep the project state explicit:

- **Proposed** — conversation or idea only.
- **Awaiting response** — participation or rights have not been confirmed.
- **Active** — written scope and responsibilities are confirmed.
- **Paused** — work is intentionally held.
- **Closed** — the collaboration ended or was declined.
- **Licensed** — a separate written license authorizes specified use.

Do not silently change one state into another.

## Public presentation

Until participation and rights are expressly confirmed, public materials should use factual language such as:

> Development reference only. No affiliation, endorsement, participation, or music rights are implied.

Do not use "official collaboration," "music by Ren," or similar language without the corresponding written basis.

## Evidence packet

For any future collaboration or licensed music use, preserve:

- dated brief/assignment;
- relevant communications;
- contributor identity and role;
- source/provenance record;
- written permission or executed agreement;
- exact approved assets/recordings;
- compensation terms, if any;
- credit language;
- media, territory, term, and use scope;
- withdrawal/termination/takedown terms where applicable.

**Principle:** Nothing downstream should erase the upstream record.
