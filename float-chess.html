Float Chess

A browser demo for simultaneous-move chess. Select a white piece, choose a destination, and commit; the black opponent submits a move from the same pre-turn position.

Select a white piece to start.

Variant actions

Select a special move to test its rule. The move must still be legal for the selected piece.
