Playable mechanics test

Float Cards · generic rules layer

Each side receives six cards from a shuffled standard 52-card deck and begins with 10 Resolve Points (RP). Commit one card each round. The cards reveal together; the higher rank deals 1 RP damage. Ties deal no damage.

01 · COMMITChoose one card from your hand each round. Used cards cannot be replayed.
02 · REVEALRanks run from 2 (lowest) to Ace (highest). Suits have no effect in this ruleset.
03 · ENDGAMEZero RP loses. If both hands run out first, higher remaining RP wins; equal RP is a draw.
