Rules of the table

Each side receives six cards from a shuffled standard 52-card deck and begins with 10 Resolve Points (RP). Choose one card; your opponent commits one too. Both are revealed together. The higher rank deals 1 RP damage. Ties deal no damage.

01 · COMMITChoose one card from your hand each round. You cannot reuse it.
02 · REVEALRank decides the round: 2 is lowest, Ace is highest. Suits have no mechanical effect in this version.
03 · SURVIVEReach 0 RP to lose. If the hands run out first, the higher remaining RP wins.
